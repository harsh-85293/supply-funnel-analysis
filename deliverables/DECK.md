## Slide 3 — Cheap phones fail on paper documents, not on cards

![](outputs/charts/device_document_gap.png)

The three affected documents are full-page paper covered in small print. The three unaffected ones are pocket-sized laminated cards. **46% of our signups are on basic handsets.**

- **Captains who try again mostly get through** — 79% eventually pass the Registration Certificate
- **Our rejection message does no work:** captains retry about 62% of the time whichever of the seven reasons they were given. "Blurred photo" and "document expired" produce identical behaviour.
- **Tested and rejected:** verification speed is not the problem. Checked within 2 hours, captains continue at 84.0%; after more than a day, 84.1%. Do not fund a queue-speed project.

![](outputs/charts/campaign_ladder.png)

**It only goes out once a captain has cleared two documents — none of the 7,981 recipients got it earlier**, and clearing the Licence and Registration Certificate is where 44% of our loss happens.

## Slide 5 — The airport isn't short of captains. It's short of captains at night.

![](outputs/charts/airport_hour_profile.png)

- Terminals fill **59.8%** of requests against **96–97%** everywhere else in the city — but **86% of that shortfall is 8pm–4am**
- **48,720 captain-hours going spare** in hours already covered, against a **17,979-hour shortfall** at night — three times more waste than shortage
- One extra captain online at night converts to **1.31 extra rides**; at midday, **0.19**

**And more captains would worsen why they stay away.** Four in ten airport trips end in a suburb 23 km out:

| Airport trip ends in | Share | Another fare within 20 min | Captain declines | Pay per hour it ties them up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| City centre | 42% | 53% | 8.6% | ₹263 |
| **Suburb (23 km out)** | **41%** | **17%** | **21.0%** | **₹176** |

**Acquisition buys headcount; it cannot buy hours** — and ~a quarter of "unmet" airport demand is a captain who accepted, then cancelled.

**Not doing:** activation work (98.7% already drive) · verification speed (tested, no effect) · city programmes (2.9 points apart, vs 8.8 across handsets) · a blanket suburban bonus (nine to twelve times the fare per extra ride) · airport-catchment acquisition

**Total onboarding prize: +183 to +344 approvals a month on a base of 728 — and every point converts almost one-for-one into a driving captain.**
